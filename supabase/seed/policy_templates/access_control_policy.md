## Purpose

Define the access-control expectations required to satisfy ECC domain 2
(Cybersecurity Defence) controls, in particular identity management,
privileged access, authentication, and audit trails.

## Scope

Applies to every information system, application, network device, and
cloud workload owned or operated by the organisation.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- CISO: owns this policy and approves exceptions.
- IT Operations: implements technical controls.
- Internal Audit: verifies compliance.

## Policy Statements

- Each user is uniquely identified; shared accounts are prohibited.
- Access rights follow the principle of least privilege and are reviewed
  quarterly.
- Privileged access uses multi-factor authentication and session
  recording.

## Enforcement

Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, loss of access, and in
severe cases legal action.

## Review Cycle

This policy is reviewed annually or when a material change in the
regulatory landscape or the organisation's risk posture occurs.
